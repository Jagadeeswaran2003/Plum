# UK Bank Data Dashboard
This dashboard provides a visual analysis of UK bank transaction data across gender, age, region, balance, and job classification. Males account for slightly more transactions than females, with 53.94% of the total. The most active age group for transactions is between 30 and 40 years, peaking around age 35. Regional analysis shows England leading with 2,159 transactions, while Scotland and Northern Ireland show significantly lower activity. Most account balances fall under 100,000, with a high concentration below 50,000. This suggests that the majority of customers maintain moderate savings. The white-collar job category leads in transaction volume, followed by blue-collar and other classifications. This implies that individuals in professional roles are more financially active. The age and job trends align, indicating that working professionals are driving bank activity. Geographic distribution highlights economic concentration in southern UK regions. Overall, the data shows that young, working males in urban regions contribute most to banking transactions.
